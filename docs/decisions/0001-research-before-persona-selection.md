## Context

Should Nest focus on individual writers and creators, or also include information gathering and everyday organization? Competitor features alone cannot determine user priorities.

## Decision

Proposed: investigate concrete jobs for several personas within existing capabilities first. Do not begin new external operations or UX reimplementation. The primary persona, first job and required schedule information have not been selected.

This remains a product-research proposal. Approval of the documentation-management convention and merging a research PR do not accept this proposal. Acceptance would not establish implementation.

## Consequences

Use J1–J5 to investigate who wants repeated work, whether next-run and stop behavior are understood, and whether reusing a Bot role has value. Revise the hypothesis if findings do not support a writer focus. Research requires time, but avoids treating feature breadth as evidence of user need.

Muse has an official introduction; Dot, Grok Bot and ASIST have official descriptions. ASIST UI findings come from a prior limited local evaluation. Instinct identity and user needs remain unconfirmed.

## Alternatives

| Option | Benefit | Cost / uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus immediately on writers | Easier to define an initial value proposition | Need and frequency are unvalidated |
| Study several personas using common journeys | Allows comparison of concrete problems | Requires research time |
| Expand general-purpose features first | Broadens apparent use cases | May leave first value and safe scope unclear |
