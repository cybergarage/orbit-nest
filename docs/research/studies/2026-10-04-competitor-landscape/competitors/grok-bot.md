# Grok Bot

Observed: 2026-10-04 UTC / 2026-10-05 JST. App build unknown. G1 updated 2026-09-29; G2 updated 2026-09-21. Evidence: documented, plus observed public website for G3. No hands-on installed-app test.

## Facts

[G1] Bots have names and jobs. Duplicate carries configuration but excludes conversation history and learned memory. A shared template creates a separate copy.

[G2] Conversations and context are separate per Bot, but files, sessions and logins on the computer are shared. Cloud work continues independently of the client, and Bot handoffs are described.

[G3] The public Marketplace displays search, use-case categories and character cards. Behavior after Add was not tested.

## Evaluation

The distinction between a role blueprint and a working instance is useful comparison material. Separate names do not imply isolated resources, which matters when explaining scope in Nest. Investigate work that benefits from handoffs before making multiple Bots an end in themselves.

## Routines, delegation, models

[G4] Routines describe schedules and supported events, an owning Bot, timezone, next run, and edit/pause/test/history controls. Run now alone does not verify scheduled execution.

[G5] Asynchronous messages wake another Bot; groups of two to six Bots and handoffs are described. Distinguish a user-managed roster from internal subagents. A durable global Kanban is unconfirmed in the reviewed material.
