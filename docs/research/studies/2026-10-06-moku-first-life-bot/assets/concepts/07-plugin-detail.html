Receipts and privacy

Proposed: keep minimal local plugin audit metadata for 30 days, with a visible delete control. Do not retain message bodies or credentials in receipts. Do not discard unresolved execution evidence automatically; review its safe retention separately. Privacy and credential-storage feasibility remain open. An uncertain send is Unknown; verify before any retry.

Proposed enforcement lives outside the model, at execution time. This design grants no actual access and introduces no runtime plugin, external write or send capability.
